FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Does GiftVault work offline if the celebration venue has poor mobile network? ▼
Yes, absolutely! GiftVault is built with offline-first local storage architecture. Even if your hall or mandapam has zero mobile network, the reception counter operates seamlessly, saving every guest entry safely on your local device.
Can I download and export the complete celebration ledger to Excel and PDF? ▼
Yes. Click 'Export CSV' in the ledger tab to instantly download an Excel-ready UTF-8 BOM spreadsheet with all guest details.
Can multiple family counters log gift envelopes at the same time? ▼
Yes. Multi-desk synchronization allows groom-side and bride-side counters to log entries simultaneously without duplicate serial numbers.
How does 2-inch thermal slip printing work? ▼
Simply connect any standard 2-inch (58mm or 80mm) Bluetooth or USB thermal receipt printer and click 'Print Slip' to print.
